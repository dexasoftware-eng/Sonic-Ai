Dual verification

Why two models beat one

Using two independently-trained models means the system is far less likely to be fooled by one model's blind spot. If they agree, confidence is high. If they disagree, that disagreement itself routes the clip to a human.

    How it works
  • Neither model sees the other's answer before it decides
  • Agreement raises confidence; disagreement routes to review

The process

How detection works in four steps

From raw audio to confirmed alert — every clip goes through the same rigorous pipeline, automatically, day and night.

01

Capture

Audio arrives via file upload or live microphone stream. It is validated for format, cleaned of artefacts, and sliced into consistent 2-second windows before any analysis begins.

Input · Validation · Segmentation
02

Compare

Two independently trained models — a Python classifier and a Google Teachable Machine model — each label the same clip without seeing each other's answer. Their predictions are then matched.

Dual AI · Independence · Cross-check
03

Decide

Configurable safety rules assign a severity level to the agreed label. Confirmed critical events fire an instant alert; uncertain or conflicting results are routed to the human review queue.

Rules engine · Severity · Routing
04

Record

Every audio event, confidence score, model prediction and human decision is stored in MongoDB — fully searchable, exportable to CSV, and auditable by anyone with the right access level.

MongoDB · Audit trail · Export
