Security Quota & Limitation Policies
Platform hard boundaries automatically enforced at the API and model inference layer.
1. Acoustic Inference Quotas

Every sound classification, mic stream, or audio upload consumes 1 credit. When a company or individual hits their monthly allowance, the inference engine rejects additional audio with HTTP 403 (QUOTA_EXCEEDED) until an upgrade is completed.

2. Staff Seat Boundaries

Company tenants are constrained by max_staff_seats. Inviting Security Operators, Maintenance Staff, or Reviewers beyond plan capacity is blocked at the invitation API.

3. Sensor Zone Allocations

Physical sensor registration and IoT endpoints are gated by max_zones. Enterprise clients with higher hardware density must subscribe to Creator (20 zones) or Pro (unlimited).
