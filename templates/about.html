Our Engineering Code

Built on deterministic physics, not generative hallucinations

Critical infrastructure security cannot rely on probabilistic text completions or opaque cloud APIs. Every line of code at Dectus adheres to strict sovereign engineering mandates.

Principle 01 // Determinism

Physics-Grounded Math

We do not ask black-box language models to guess whether a sound is a gunshot. We decompose acoustics into mathematical Fourier transforms, log-mel energy distribution tensors, and transient frequency centroids.

Principle 02 // Absolute Privacy

Irreversible On-Chip Anonymization

Audio streams are converted to acoustic spectral vectors within memory buffers on the edge gateway and immediately flushed. Speech formants are stripped mathematically, rendering eavesdropping impossible.

Principle 03 // Total Sovereignty

Zero Mandatory Cloud Link

Dectus runs 100% air-gapped on your local hardware. If an international fiber cable is cut or your facility enters lockdown, your acoustic security alerts and industrial automation continue without disruption.

Principle 04 // Microsecond Reflexes

Ultra-Low Latency Execution

Compiled in Rust and modern C++ utilizing ARM NEON SIMD and NVIDIA TensorRT. Threat adjudication finishes in under 40 milliseconds — 100x faster than cloud round-trips.
